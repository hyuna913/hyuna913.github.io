Hyunah Lim

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at the University of Maryland Institute for Health Computing (UM-IHC).
My PhD work at the University of Maryland focused on mathematical modeling of tumor growth and treatment response, particularly incorporating spatial heterogeneity such as drug resistance and cell life cycles. I am currently interested in advancing these models toward predicting treatment outcomes and resistance evolution, with an emphasis on integrating imaging data and quantitative inference.
